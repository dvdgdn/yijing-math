# Claims to Verify (Phase 1: statements and definitions only)

The claims below were reported by an earlier investigation in this program. **None has been independently verified.** You receive only their statements and the definitions they use. The original reasoning and evidence will be supplied later, after you have recorded your own derivations.

## Conventions used by these claims

You may work in any representation you prefer, but you must check the claims as stated in this one.

- **Encoding.** A hexagram is a word x = (x₁, …, x₆) with entries in {0, 1}, read from bottom (x₁) to top (x₆), with unbroken (yang) = 1 and broken (yin) = 0. Addition ⊕ is coordinatewise mod 2; the words form a 6-dimensional vector space over the two-element field.
- **R (reversal):** R(x₁, …, x₆) = (x₆, …, x₁). **C (complement):** C(x) = x ⊕ 111111.
- **Figures:** the orbits of the group generated by R: pairs {x, Rx} with Rx ≠ x, and single hexagrams fixed by R.
- **Canons:** the upper canon is hexagrams 1 to 30 of the received (King Wen) order; the lower canon is 31 to 64.
- **Pairs:** the 32 consecutive pairs {2k−1, 2k} of the received order. A separate verification is checking the reported fact that each pair is either {x, Rx} with Rx ≠ x, or {x, Cx} with Rx = x. You may rely on it, but check it if it matters to your verdict.
- **Nuclear map:** N(x) = (x₂, x₃, x₄, x₃, x₄, x₅), the lower trigram taken from lines 2–4 and the upper from lines 3–5.
- **A:** the set {000000, 111111, 101010, 010101}.
- **Hamming distance:** the number of lines in which two hexagrams differ.
- **Correct position:** a line is in correct position if it is yang in an odd position (1, 3, 5) or yin in an even position (2, 4, 6).
- **Pair-shuffling null model:** random sequences obtained by permuting the 32 pairs uniformly at random and choosing the order within each pair uniformly at random. Where a claim below is ambiguous about the model or the statistic, state each reasonable reading and give a verdict for each.

## Claims

**C-0003.** The upper canon and the lower canon each contain exactly 18 figures.

**C-0004.** If the upper canon consists of p pairs of R-fixed hexagrams and r reversal pairs and contains 18 figures, then 2p + r = 18 and the upper canon has 36 − 2p hexagrams; with p = 3 (pairs 1/2, 27/28, 29/30) this gives 30.

**C-0005.** Under the pair-shuffling null model, the probability that the first 15 pairs contain exactly 3 pairs of R-fixed hexagrams is about 22%.

**C-0006.** Of the eight doubled-trigram hexagrams, the upper canon contains exactly those whose trigram is fixed by reversal (Qian 1, Kun 2, Kan 29, Li 30), and the lower canon those whose trigram is not (Zhen 51, Gen 52, Xun 57, Dui 58).

**C-0007.** N is linear, commutes with R and with C, and satisfies N⁴ = N². Its minimal polynomial is t²(t + 1)².

**C-0008.** The space of words is the direct sum of ker N² = {x : x₃ = x₄ = 0} (dimension 4) and A. Every hexagram enters A within two applications of N. On A, N fixes 000000 and 111111 and swaps 101010 and 010101.

**C-0009.** In the received order, the four elements of A are hexagrams 1, 2, 63, 64.

**C-0010.** Under the pair-shuffling null model, the probability that both pairs contained in A ({1, 2} and {63, 64}) occupy the two end positions of the sequence is 0.0021. Conditional on the pair {1, 2} occupying the first position, the probability is 1/31.

**C-0011.** (a) The number of hexagrams whose nuclear hexagram N(x) lies in the same canon as x is 38 in the received order, with null probability (p-value) 0.29 under pair-shuffling. (b) The number of hexagrams whose nuclear hexagram lies in the same pair or an adjacent pair is 6, with p-value 0.87.

**C-0012.** A is exactly the set of words that are constant, or have all six lines in correct position, or have all six lines in incorrect position.

**C-0013.** The Hamming distance between the two members of each pair is even.

**C-0014.** For the 31 transitions from hexagram 2k to hexagram 2k + 1 (k = 1, …, 31), the Hamming distances have distribution 1:2, 2:8, 3:13, 4:7, 6:1. None equals 5.

**C-0015.** Under pair-shuffling, the probability that no between-pair transition has Hamming distance 5 is 0.043.

**C-0026 (restricted to the claims above).** Each of the claims above holds equally if the labels are swapped (yin = 1, yang = 0).
