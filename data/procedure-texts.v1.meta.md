# procedure-texts.v1.csv

Verbatim excerpts of texts describing the yarrow-stalk procedure and the coin method. One row per witness. The `text` column holds the excerpt.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Source | SRC-007. zh.wikisource.org, retrieved 2026-11-01 at the revisions in `revision_url`. Rows: (1) *Xici* 上, `易傳/繫辭上`; (2) *Xici* 上, the classic-text segments of `周易正義/07.08`; (3) Zhu Xi, 筮儀, `原本周易本義 (四庫全書本)/卷末下`; (4) Zhu Xi, 明筮, `原本周易本義 (四庫全書本)/卷末上`; (5) Jia Gongyan's subcommentary on the *Yili*, `儀禮註疏/卷一`; (6) Gu Yanwu, `日知錄/卷15`, quoting row 5. |
| Transcriber | Claude Opus 5.5, INV-0008, by script (`compute/experiments/INV-0008/build_baselines.py`). |
| Date | 2026-11-01 |
| Checking status | `unchecked`. Rows 1 and 2 are two witnesses of one passage. Rows 3–6 have one witness each. |
| Conventions | Excerpts are verbatim spans between fixed start and end strings. Wiki markup is rendered as follows: `{{gap}}` and line breaks removed; small-character notes (`{{SK notes|…}}`, `{{注|…}}`) shown as 〔…〕; Siku glyph images shown as `[SKchar:N]`. Row 2 joins the segments that the transcription marks as classic text (`{{yw|…}}`), omitting commentary. |
| Known variants | Rows 1 and 2 arrange the passage differently: row 1 places the sentence on the numbers of heaven and earth (天數…行鬼神也) before 大衍之數五十; row 2 places 天數五，地數五… after 故再扐而後掛. Row 3 reads 中分四中九䇿 where 四十九 is expected; not corrected. |
