## Statement

If the upper canon consists of p palindrome pairs and r reversal pairs and contains 18 figures, then 2p + r = 18, so the upper canon has 2p + 2r = 36 − 2p hexagrams. With p = 3 (pairs 1/2, 27/28, 29/30) this gives 30, the size of the upper canon.

The source presents this as explaining "the odd 30/34 split".

## Definitions and assumptions

Assumes the canon boundary falls between pairs and that each canon contains 18 figures (C-0003). The p = 3 value comes from the received order (C-0002).

## Scope and limitations

The arithmetic is conditional on balance. It explains where the cut falls *given* balance and p = 3; it does not explain why balance holds or why p = 3. INV-0001 §6 says the same: "what it explains is why the cut is at 30, not why the order looks the way it does."
