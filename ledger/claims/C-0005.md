## Statement

Under the pair-shuffling null model, the probability that the first 15 pairs contain exactly p = 3 palindrome pairs (equivalently, that the upper canon of 15 pairs is balanced at 18 figures) is about 22%. Canon balance is therefore not rare under this model.

Original wording: "Within the pair-shuffling model, the canon balance itself is not rare, with about a 22% chance of p = 3 in the first 15 pairs."

## Definitions and assumptions

DEF-013 (null model). Assumes the cut falls after 15 pairs.

## Scope and limitations

Concerns one null model and one statistic.

Not ruled out: that balance reflects a deliberate constraint (rarity is not the only possible evidence); that balance is notable under other null models (e.g. ones respecting other structure in the order); that balance combined with C-0006 is jointly notable.

## Objections and alternatives

Registrar note: under the stated model this probability should be exactly computable (a hypergeometric count of palindrome pairs among 15 of 32 pairs); the verifier should compute it exactly.
