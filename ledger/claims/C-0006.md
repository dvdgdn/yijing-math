## Statement

Of the eight doubled-trigram hexagrams (elements of P), the upper canon of the received order contains exactly the four whose trigram is itself a palindrome, Qian, Kun, Kan, Li (hexagrams 1, 2, 29, 30), and the lower canon contains exactly the four whose trigram is not, Zhen, Gen, Xun, Dui (hexagrams 51, 52, 57, 58).

DEF-009 (P), DEF-012 (canons). Data: received order (SRC-001).
