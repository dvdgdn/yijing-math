## Statement

GF(2)⁶ = ker N² ⊕ A, where ker N² = {x : x₃ = x₄ = 0} has dimension 4 and A = {000000, 111111, 101010, 010101} is the set of words of period dividing 2. Every hexagram enters A within two applications of N. On A, N fixes 000000 and 111111 and swaps 101010 and 010101.
