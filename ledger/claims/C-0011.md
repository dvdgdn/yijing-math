## Statement

INV-0001's hypothesis H4 ("the nuclear map's dynamics organize the sequence") fails beyond the endpoints, by two statistics under the pair-shuffling null model:

- *Same canon:* the number of hexagrams whose nuclear hexagram lies in the same canon is 38 for the received order, null p = 0.29.
- *Nearby pair:* the number of hexagrams whose nuclear hexagram lies in the same or an adjacent pair is 6, null p = 0.87.
