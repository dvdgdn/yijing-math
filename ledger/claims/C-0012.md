## Statement

The period-2 words A = {000000, 111111, 101010, 010101} are exactly the words that are constant, or have all six lines in correct position, or all six in incorrect position. Hence N is only one of several operators or criteria that single out A, and the endpoint placement (C-0009) is "equally explained by a competitor".

## Definitions and assumptions

DEF-014, DEF-016. The traditional notion of correct position is used informally in the source; DEF-016 is the registrar's gloss.

## Scope and limitations

The set identity is formal. The inference "equally explained" is an interpretive judgment about competing explanations of C-0009, recorded in DSP-001.

## Objections and alternatives

Other characterizations of A may exist; the space of "natural" criteria singling out these four hexagrams has not been surveyed.

## Further investigation
