## Statement

Under pair-shuffling, the probability of no distance-5 transition between pairs is 0.043. This is before correcting for the many statistics one could have tested, so it is not significant. INV-0001 concludes that H3 ("Hamming distances between consecutive hexagrams carry design") "is not earned".
