## Statement

For figures of 2k lines, there are (2^{2k} + 2^k)/2 R-orbits ("figures"), and a nuclear map generalizes with attractors at the period-2 words. INV-0001 concludes: "Six lines is not mathematically special. What is distinctive is which choices the texts made."

Registered as a conjecture because the generalized nuclear map is not defined in the source; the orbit count is a standard Burnside-type count and may be routinely provable.

## Scope and limitations

The statement is ill-posed until a generalized nuclear map is specified. Several natural generalizations exist, and the attractor claim may hold for some and not others.
