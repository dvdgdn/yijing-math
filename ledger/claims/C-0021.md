## Statement

INV-0001's "ambitious conjecture" (every traditional structure is a V-equivariant affine operator on GF(2)⁶, so that the Yijing would be a representation of V with a positional grading) is false. Counterexample: the Jing Fang palace construction, encoded by D, is not R-equivariant; Corollary B (C-0019) is "precisely the trace of the broken symmetry". By contrast the received pairing and N commute with R.

## Definitions and assumptions

DEF-008, DEF-011. Depends on the encoding D being correct (C-0017). "Traditional structure" and "affine operator" are not defined precisely in the source; the palace system is a partition with labels, not an operator, so the refuted statement must be read loosely.

## Scope and limitations

Refutes one formulation of one unification proposal, under one encoding.

Not ruled out: equivariance of traditional structures under other groups or other actions; a unification restricted to some layers (see C-0022); unifications in other encodings or other mathematical frameworks; the possibility that the palace *partition* (as opposed to its generation labels) has symmetry properties of its own. This result does not count against symmetry-based approaches in general.

## Objections and alternatives

The conjecture was imprecise, so its refutation is correspondingly informal. A precise restatement would clarify what exactly is refuted.
