## Statement

INV-0001's baseline H0: the 64 hexagrams form the 6-cube with no further structure, and the received order is arbitrary once the pairs are fixed.

Registrar's restatement for testing: under H0, the order of the 32 pairs and their orientation are statistically indistinguishable from the pair-shuffling null model with respect to pre-specified statistics.

## Definitions and assumptions

Pair-shuffling null model (DEF-013). The registrar's restatement makes H0 testable but is narrower than the source's wording.

## Provenance

- INV-0001 (Claude Opus 5.5; exact model identifier unknown), §3 (H0).

## Evidence and verification

The source's tests of H3 (C-0015) and H4 beyond the endpoints (C-0011) do not reject H0. The endpoint fact (C-0010) is the main evidence against it, subject to the post hoc selection caveat. Canon balance is not rare under H0 (C-0005).

Verification log:
- None. Registered at ledger v0.1 (2026-10-31).

## Scope and limitations

A baseline, recorded so that structural claims have a stated competitor. Not a claim that the order is in fact arbitrary.
