## Statement

Let X = (L, U) be uniform on {0,1}^{2k} and Y = X ⊕ E with independent Bernoulli(p) noise bits, 0 ≤ p ≤ 1/2. Put D(X) = L ⊕ U and q = 2p(1−p). For every Boolean function g on k bits, I(g(D(X)); Y) = I(g(D(X)); D(Y)). Assuming Corollary `main:ck` of external family 119, I(g(D(X)); Y) ≤ 1 − h₂(q), attained by one coordinate of D. For 0 < p < 1/2 this is strictly less than the 1 − h₂(p) retained by one raw line.

## Definitions and assumptions

DEF-001, DEF-004, DEF-010 (D = φ), DEF-017 (symmetric line-change channel). Uniform input and independent symmetric noise.

## Evidence and verification

Reported: transfer proof in source (coordinate change (L,U) ↦ (D,U); U(Y) independent of (D, D(Y))); exhaustive check of all 256 functions g at k = 3, p = 1/8 and 1/4.

Verification log:
- 2026-10-31, INV-0004 (Claude Opus 5.5, context-exposed, own code): reduction identity recomputed for all 254 nonconstant g at both p, maximum discrepancy below 2 × 10⁻¹⁵. Exactly 6 functions attain 1 − h₂(q). Agreement. Not a protocol verification (no isolation); status unchanged.

## Scope and limitations

The all-dimension optimum depends on the external theorem, which is not audited by the programme; arXiv:2609.26444 claims an independent proof (abstract only read, INV-0004 §7). No asymmetric or biased-input statement. At p = 1/4 the channel is the coins model (C-0032).

## Objections and alternatives

The reduction is an elementary change of variables; the substance is the imported theorem.
