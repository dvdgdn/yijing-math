## Statement

With X uniform on six lines and symmetric line-change noise p: I(B(X);Y) ≈ 1.103576694734 and I(G(X);Y) ≈ 0.726364612385 bits at p = 1/8; 0.378621055176 and 0.136697991225 bits at p = 1/4. B and G are independent uniform eight-valued labels. A raw trigram retains 3[1 − h₂(p)], more than either at these two values.

## Definitions and assumptions

DEF-011, DEF-017, DEF-018. B and G are built from the specified set D; correspondence with a cited palace table is C-0017.

- INV-0003 §3 (local result L2).

## Evidence and verification

Reported: exact rational posterior masses, binary64 logarithms; hashes in INV-0003 `pilot-results.json`.

Verification log:
- 2026-10-31, INV-0004 (Claude Opus 5.5, context-exposed, own code): all four values agree to at least 12 significant digits; independence of B and G confirmed. Not a protocol verification.

## Scope and limitations

Two noise values only. Generalized to every 0 < p < 1/2 by C-0035, which does not supersede this record because the exact values remain useful fixtures. At p = 1/4 the channel is the coins model (C-0032).

## Objections and alternatives

INV-0003 called the channel an artificial model choice; C-0032 identifies it with the coins model.
