## Statement

Join each word x of length 2k to C(x) and, when different, to R(x). The graph splits into 2^k two-vertex components and 2^(2k−2) − 2^(k−1) four-cycles (the ⟨R, C⟩-orbits). Hence it has exactly 2^(2^(2k−2) − 2^(k−1)) perfect matchings: 4,096 for six lines.

## Definitions and assumptions

DEF-002, DEF-003, DEF-008. The weak compatibility rule only (reversal or complement); the received rule preferring reversal picks one matching.
