## Statement

On 64 positions labelled by six-bit words, swap each pair along coordinate 1 independently with probability 1/2, then coordinate 2, …, coordinate 6. Every label ends exactly uniform over positions, yet the permutation uses at most 192 fair bits, so its total-variation distance from the uniform law on S₆₄ is at least 1 − 2^192/64! = 1 − 2^(−103.995…). At least ten physical Thorp shuffles are needed for distance 1/4.

## Scope and limitations

## Objections and alternatives

The marginal-versus-joint gap is generic for low-randomness permutations; its relevance to any attested order is unestablished.
