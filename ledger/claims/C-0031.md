## Statement

For any ordering x₀, …, x₆₃ of all hexagrams and any nonzero linear character a, the signs sⱼ = (−1)^(a·xⱼ) sum to zero, so P(z) = Σ sⱼ zʲ vanishes at z = 1. The positive lower envelope used by external family 076's ultraflat construction therefore cannot hold for these sequences.

## Definitions and assumptions

DEF-001; linear characters over the six bits.

## Scope and limitations

Refutes only this direct transfer.

Not ruled out: spectral questions away from z = 1, aperiodic correlations, nonlinear or biased observables, incomplete arrangements, other sequence alphabets.
