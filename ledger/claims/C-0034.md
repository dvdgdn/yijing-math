## Statement

Assume Corollary `main:ck` of external family 119. Under the coins model, every deterministic Boolean function f of the primary hexagram satisfies I(f(primary); changed) ≤ 1 − h₂(1/4) ≈ 0.188722 bits, with equality for a single line. The same bound holds for questions about the changed hexagram regarding the primary (the joint law is exchangeable). A question with output mean m (sign convention) obeys the refined bound ψ(r_m/2)/ln 2 with r_m = ψ⁻¹(H(m)).
