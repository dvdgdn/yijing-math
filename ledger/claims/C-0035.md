## Statement

Let X be uniform on six lines and Y = X ⊕ E with independent Bernoulli(p) noise, 0 < p < 1/2. Then I(T(X);Y) > I(B(X);Y) > I(G(X);Y), where T is the lower trigram (equivalently any three lines), B the palace label and G the generation label. At p = 1/4 (the coins model) the values are 0.566166, 0.378621 and 0.136698 bits.

## Evidence and verification

Reported. B vs G: Fano bound for p ≤ 2⁻¹⁰; I(B;Y) ≥ 1 − h₂(p) via line 6 for p ≥ 0.23; 30-interval monotone grid between (smallest margin 0.0102). T vs B: conditioning on at most one flip for p ≤ 2⁻¹⁴; χ²/Kullback–Leibler bound with exact Fourier levels for p ≥ 0.48475; 233-interval grid between (smallest margin 5.4 × 10⁻⁴). Monotonicity from data processing.

Verification log:
- None. Prepared for ASN-0014.

## Scope and limitations

Symmetric noise only; yarrow values (asymmetric) are computed at specific models but not covered by the theorem. Grid logarithms are binary64 libm calls with margin 10⁻¹⁰, not interval arithmetic. Settles the all-noise question left open by INV-0003 §3 and ASN-0010.

## Objections and alternatives

The ordering is explained structurally by C-0036 (edge boundary at low noise, level-1 Fourier weight at high noise); significance for historical design is not claimed.
