## Statement

For every 0 < p < 1/2 and Y = X ⊕ E with symmetric noise, no balanced eight-valued label of six lines retains more information about Y than a partition into coordinate 3-subcubes.

## Definitions and assumptions

DEF-018 (balanced labels).

## Provenance

- INV-0004 R6; `results.json` key `partition_search`. The general statement for 2 ≤ k ≤ 7 is conjectured in arXiv:2609.26444 (abstract only read), which also reports a coding-based counterexample for k ≥ 8. This record is the n = 6, k = 3 instance, not a new conjecture.

## Evidence and verification

Reported: endpoint optimality (C-0036); the trigram is a strict local maximum under single swaps at p = 1/10, p = 1/4 and the yarrow-standard channel (best change −0.0196, −0.0107, −0.0118 bits); annealing from the palace partition and three random partitions (60,000 swaps each) never exceeded the trigram.

Verification log:
- None.

## Scope and limitations

Balanced labels only. Local search is not exhaustive.

Not ruled out: a distant non-affine balanced label or an unbalanced label exceeding the trigram at intermediate p.

## Objections and alternatives

The k ≥ 8 counterexample shows coordinate optimality is not automatic for multi-bit labels.
