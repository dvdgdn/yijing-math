## Statement

Under the yarrow-standard, yarrow-uniform and yarrow-binomial models, for six lines, the most informative deterministic yes/no question about the primary hexagram regarding the changed one, and about the changed hexagram regarding the primary, is a single line.

## Definitions and assumptions

DEF-017. The external theorem does not apply: the channels are asymmetric and some inputs are biased.

## Provenance

- INV-0004 R7; `results.json` keys `dictator_scans`, `dictator_n6`.

## Evidence and verification

Reported: for n = 1, …, 4 the maximum over all nonconstant Boolean functions is attained only by the 2n signed single-line functions, in all eight model/direction cases (gap at n = 4 between 0.008 and 0.028 bits); at n = 6 local search never beat a single line.

Verification log:
- None.

## Scope and limitations

Evidence, not proof. Novelty unknown: a short search found no treatment of asymmetric channels.

Not ruled out: a non-coordinate optimum at five or six lines escaping local search; failure for more extreme asymmetric channels.

## Objections and alternatives

None recorded.
