## Statement

In both digital witnesses of the *Xugua* (序卦), `易傳/序卦` (Wikisource revision 2611127) and `周易正義/10` (revision 2384519), the hexagram names introduced by 受之以 are 61 distinct hexagrams. In order of first mention, they are received numbers 3–30 followed by 32–64. The *Xugua* names 乾 and 坤 only as 天地, and opens its lower part without naming 咸.

## Definitions and assumptions

SRC-001; `data/zhouyi-received-order.v1.csv` (D-1) for the numbering. One witness reads 故受之既濟 without 以. The other runs Han Kangbo's commentary into the text; only first mentions count.
