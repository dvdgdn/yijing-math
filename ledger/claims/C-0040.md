## Statement

In the two digital witnesses of the *Jingshi Yizhuan* (J1: Wikisource `京氏易傳`, revision 7903767; J2: `京氏易傳 (四庫全書本)` 卷上 and 卷中, revisions 1562382 and 657265):

- The 64 entries agree in sequence, name (after a stated seven-variant normalization) and trigram composition.
- They form eight consecutive blocks of eight, each beginning with a doubled trigram, in the order 乾 震 坎 艮 坤 巽 離 兌. They cover all 64 hexagrams.
- In every block, the line the text names as holding the world (世) is 6, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 4, 3 for positions 0–7.

## Definitions and assumptions

SRC-002; `data/jingshi-yizhuan-eight-palaces.v1.csv` (D-2). The world line is read from J1's phrase, with its J2 reading checked. 57 phrases contain 世. 7 say which line 居尊/處至尊/立元首 (震, 井, 大過, 節, 噬嗑, 旅, 渙). 蒙 reads 諸位立世 in both witnesses and relies on the editor's emendation to 諸侯, or on the following 元士為應. 乾 is a manual reading of a note.

## Scope and limitations

Two digital witnesses, possibly not independent transcriptions. No printed edition was checked. Says nothing about attribution to Jing Fang or the date of the text.

## Objections and alternatives

Indirect phrases and the 蒙 emendation are interpretive readings. Excluding those eight entries leaves 56 explicit agreements.
