## Statement

Zhu Xi's 筮儀 (Siku transcription), as quoted in `data/procedure-texts.v1.csv`:

- Remainders are 或一或二或三或四.
- The hung stalk is taken from the right heap.
- In the first change, the left/right remainder pairs give a total of 5 or 9, counted 奇者三而偶者一 (three to one).
- In the second and third changes, totals of 4 or 8 are counted 奇偶各得四之二 (two to two).
- Totals of 13, 17, 21 and 25 stalks are 老陽, 少陰, 少陽 and 老陰.

Weighting the counted cases equally and treating the three changes independently gives P(6, 7, 8, 9) = (1/16, 5/16, 7/16, 3/16), the yarrow-standard model of DEF-017. Neither the 筮儀 nor Zhu Xi's 明筮 (信手平分) states a distribution for the split of the stalks.

## Definitions and assumptions

DEF-017. Equal weighting of the counted cases is an interpretive step; the text counts cases and does not assert probabilities.

## Scope and limitations

One witness of one Song-dynasty description. Does not show that practitioners' splits make the cases equally likely; under the uniform-split model of C-0033 they are not. Does not concern the coin method. Jia Gongyan's Tang account gives coin outcome names (重/交/單/拆) without a mechanism or probabilities.

## Objections and alternatives

The case count may be a mnemonic tally rather than a probability claim.
