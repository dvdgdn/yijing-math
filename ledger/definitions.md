# Definitions Used by Registered Claims

Definitions and encodings referenced by claim records. **Registering a definition here does not make it privileged.** These are the choices particular investigations made; alternatives are listed where known, and new investigations are free to use different ones.

Each entry gives the definition as used, its origin, whether it encodes a historical source, and known alternatives. "Registrar note" marks comments added during registration, which are not part of the source investigation.

### DEF-001 — Hexagram as a word in GF(2)⁶

A hexagram is x = (x₁, …, x₆) ∈ GF(2)⁶, read **bottom to top**, with yang (unbroken) = 1 and yin (broken) = 0.
*Origin:* INV-0001 §1. *Encodes source:* line order follows the traditional bottom-to-top numbering; the 0/1 labels are arbitrary.
*Alternatives:* yin = 1 (label swap); top-to-bottom reading; numerical encodings (binary integer with either end most significant); four-valued divinatory lines (6/7/8/9); encodings that are not vectors at all.

### DEF-002 — Reversal R

R(x₁, …, x₆) = (x₆, …, x₁): the figure turned upside down. INV-0001: "the figure seen from the other side of the table".
*Origin:* INV-0001 §1. *Registrar note:* commonly identified with the traditional inverted hexagram (綜卦); INV-0001 does not state this identification.

### DEF-003 — Complement C

C(x) = x ⊕ 111111: every line changed.
*Origin:* INV-0001 §1. *Registrar note:* commonly identified with the traditional opposite hexagram (錯卦); INV-0001 does not state this identification.

### DEF-004 — Trigram decomposition

x = (l, u), with lower trigram l = (x₁, x₂, x₃) and upper trigram u = (x₄, x₅, x₆), each in GF(2)³.
*Origin:* INV-0001 §1. *Encodes source:* the traditional lower/upper trigram reading.

### DEF-005 — Nuclear map N

N(x) = (x₂, x₃, x₄, x₃, x₄, x₅): lower trigram from lines 2–4, upper trigram from lines 3–5.
*Origin:* INV-0001 §3 (H4) and Theorem 3. *Encodes source:* the traditional nuclear hexagram (互卦). *Registrar note:* traditional usage of "nuclear" figures varies (e.g. nuclear trigrams 互體 used individually); this definition is one standard form.

### DEF-006 — Pairing involution σ

σ(x) = R(x) if R(x) ≠ x, and σ(x) = C(x) otherwise.
*Origin:* INV-0001 Theorem 1.

### DEF-007 — Figures (R-orbits)

The orbits of the group ⟨R⟩ acting on GF(2)⁶: 28 two-element orbits {x, Rx} with Rx ≠ x, plus 8 palindromes, 36 in all.
*Origin:* INV-0001 Theorem 2.

### DEF-008 — The group V = ⟨R, C⟩

The Klein four-group generated by R and C, acting on GF(2)⁶.
*Origin:* INV-0001 §3 (H2).

### DEF-009 — Doubled-trigram subspace P

P = {(t, t) : t ∈ GF(2)³}, the eight hexagrams whose upper and lower trigrams coincide.
*Origin:* INV-0001 §3 (H5) and §5.

### DEF-010 — Trigram difference map φ

φ(x) = l ⊕ u, the lower trigram XOR the upper trigram. φ: GF(2)⁶ → GF(2)³ is linear with kernel P.
*Origin:* INV-0001 Theorem 4. Positions of φ(x) are called earth (bottom), man (middle), heaven (top) in INV-0001's Corollary A.

### DEF-011 — Palace difference set D (encoding of the Jing Fang procedure)

As stated in INV-0001 §5: starting from a doubled trigram b = (t, t), generations 1–5 flip lines {1}, {1,2}, …, {1,…,5} cumulatively from the bottom; the "wandering soul" (游魂) then reverts line 4; the "returning soul" (歸魂) restores the lower trigram. The eight difference vectors, with labels:

| Label | d |
|---|---|
| pure (本宮) | 000000 |
| 1st generation | 100000 |
| 2nd generation | 110000 |
| 3rd generation | 111000 |
| 4th generation | 111100 |
| 5th generation | 111110 |
| wandering soul | 111010 |
| returning soul | 000010 |

A palace is {b ⊕ d : d ∈ D} for a fixed b ∈ P.
*Origin:* INV-0001 §5. *Encodes source:* the Jing Fang eight-palace system (SRC-002). **Whether this encoding matches the source is itself a claim (C-0017) and has not been checked.**

### DEF-012 — Canon division of the received order

Upper canon: hexagrams 1–30 of the received order; lower canon: 31–64.
*Origin:* INV-0001 §1. *Encodes source:* SRC-001 (conventional division of the transmitted text).

### DEF-013 — Pair-shuffling null model

Random orderings obtained by permuting the 32 consecutive pairs (2k−1, 2k) of the received order uniformly at random, and choosing the order within each pair uniformly at random.
*Origin:* INV-0001 §4, §6, §7. *Registrar note:* INV-0001 does not specify whether any pair is held fixed, how many samples were drawn, or whether probabilities were computed exactly or by simulation. INV-0001 itself names this null model as its weakest assumption.

### DEF-014 — Period-2 subspace A

A = {000000, 111111, 101010, 010101}: the words whose line pattern has period dividing 2. A = image of N².
*Origin:* INV-0001 Theorem 3.

### DEF-015 — Hamming distance

The number of lines in which two hexagrams differ.
*Origin:* INV-0001 §2, §4 (H3). Standard.

### DEF-016 — Correct position (當位)

A line is in correct position if it is yang in an odd position (1, 3, 5) or yin in an even position (2, 4, 6).
*Origin:* used informally in INV-0001 §4 ("all lines in correct position"). *Registrar note:* this gloss of the traditional notion was written by the registrar; INV-0001 does not define it.

### DEF-017 — Procedure models and the line-change channel

Each model generates six line values in {6, 7, 8, 9} independently. Value v gives primary line X (yang iff v ∈ {7, 9}) and change bit E (iff v ∈ {6, 9}); the changed hexagram is Y = X ⊕ E (Y = X when nothing changes, a formal convention). Models:

- **coins:** sum of three fair coins worth 2 or 3.
- **yarrow-standard:** first change removes 5 or 9 stalks with probabilities 3/4 and 1/4; the second and third remove 4 or 8 with 1/2 each.
- **yarrow-uniform:** 49 stalks; each change has left heap uniform on 1, …, n−1, one stalk hung from the right heap, both heaps counted by fours (remainder 1–4, empty heap 0).
- **yarrow-binomial:** as yarrow-uniform, with left heap Binomial(n, 1/2) conditioned on 1 ≤ L ≤ n−1.

The "symmetric line-change channel with parameter p" is Y = X ⊕ E with X uniform and the six E bits independent Bernoulli(p).
*Origin:* INV-0004 §2. *Encodes source:* the models are mathematical idealizations; their correspondence with cited descriptions of the yarrow (Xici) and three-coin procedures is unverified (ASN-0007). *Alternatives:* other split laws, hung-stalk and remainder conventions; procedures with non-independent lines.

### DEF-018 — Eight-valued labels T, B, G

For x = (l, u) (DEF-004): T(x) = l (lower trigram; any three lines are equivalent under symmetric noise); G(x) = φ(x) = l ⊕ u (DEF-010), whose bits record whether lines (1,4), (2,5), (3,6) differ; B(x) = b where x = (b, b) ⊕ d with d the unique element of D (DEF-011) satisfying φ(d) = φ(x), the base element of P (DEF-009). A label is *balanced* if each of its eight values is taken by exactly eight hexagrams.
*Origin:* INV-0003 §3 (B and G), INV-0004 §2 (T and balanced labels). *Registrar note:* the bits of G coincide with the traditional criterion for a responding (應) pair of opposite kind; this identification is general knowledge, not a cited source.

### DEF-019 — Robustness rates of a label

For a label λ with blocks A_k on six lines: β(λ) = 2|∂λ|/64, the mean number of single-line changes that alter the label (|∂λ| is the number of hypercube edges joining different blocks); W₁(λ) = Σ_k ‖s(A_k)‖²/(64|A_k|) with s_i(A) = Σ_{x∈A} (−1)^{x_i}, the level-1 Fourier weight. They govern the low-noise and high-noise behaviour of I(λ(X);Y) under the symmetric line-change channel.
*Origin:* INV-0004 R5.
