# Open Questions

Precise questions whose answers would change what the program believes or does. Each entry names its origin and related claims. A question is closed only by recording the answer (with claim IDs) under it; it is never deleted.

Status values: `open`, `assigned` (an assignment addresses it), `answered` (answer recorded), `reformulated` (replaced by a better question, linked).

### Q-001 — Do other early orderings preserve the received pairing structure?

Is the order of the Shanghai Museum Chu bamboo manuscript recoverable, wholly or in part? Where it is, does it pair hexagrams by reversal or complement as the received order does? What about other early witnesses?
*Origin:* INV-0001 §7 (decisive check for C-0022). *Related:* C-0022, DIR-005, DIR-011. *Status:* open. *Needs:* historical sources (SRC-005); not answerable by mathematics alone.

### Q-002 — What symmetry does each attested ordering and procedure have, under a pre-registered grammar of operators and statistics?

Fix in advance a small grammar of operators (e.g. reversal, complement, trigram operations) and statistics. Then classify every attested ordering and procedure (received order, Mawangdui order, Chu manuscript where recoverable, Jing Fang palaces, Shao Yong arrangements) by the symmetries it respects, accounting for the number of encodings and statistics searched.
*Origin:* INV-0001 §7 ("most informative next step"). *Related:* C-0021, C-0022, DIR-004. *Status:* open; partly addressed by ASN-0005.

### Q-003 — How special is a cumulative bottom-up rule that lands on a transversal?

Define precisely a family of "cumulative flip" procedures comparable to the Jing Fang construction (e.g. cumulative flips in some line order followed by a bounded number of correction moves). How many members of the family produce a transversal of the doubled-trigram subspace? Is the Jing Fang procedure typical, rare, or unique within natural families?
*Origin:* INV-0001 §6 remark on Theorem 4. *Related:* C-0016, C-0017. *Status:* open. Well posed and finite once the family is fixed; a good candidate for a specialist assignment after ASN-0001 reports.

### Q-004 — Which null models are appropriate for the received order, and how sensitive are the statistical claims to the choice?

Candidates include pair-shuffling, models that also preserve the canon division, models of accretion, and models constrained by other known structure. How do the conclusions of C-0005, C-0010, C-0011 and C-0015 change across them?
*Origin:* INV-0001 §7 ("weakest assumption"). *Related:* C-0005, C-0010, C-0011, C-0015, DSP-002. *Status:* assigned (ASN-0005).

### Q-005 — How does the nuclear map generalize to 2k lines, and where are its attractors?

Specify natural generalizations of the nuclear map to figures of 2k lines. For each, determine the eventual image and dynamics. Prove the orbit count (2^{2k} + 2^k)/2 for R-orbits.
*Origin:* C-0020. *Related:* C-0008, C-0020, DIR-009. *Status:* open.

### Q-006 — Which natural criteria single out hexagrams 1, 2, 63, 64?

Survey criteria (operators, line-position notions, trigram relations, others) that single out exactly these four hexagrams. What is a defensible comparison class for saying that a criterion "explains" their placement at the ends of the received order?
*Origin:* C-0012. *Related:* C-0009, C-0010, DSP-001. *Status:* assigned in part (ASN-0005).

### Q-007 — How much of the doubled-trigram canon split is implied by pairing and canon balance?

Determine which parts of C-0006 follow logically from C-0002 to C-0004 and which are additional constraints, and quantify the additional part under an appropriate null model.
*Origin:* registrar note on C-0006. *Related:* C-0003, C-0004, C-0006. *Status:* assigned (ASN-0002).

### Q-008 — Is the generation mnemonic historically independent of the palace tables?

Where and when is the mnemonic 天同二世天变五… first attested? Was it derived from the palace tables, or transmitted independently? The answer determines whether its agreement with C-0018 is independent confirmation.
*Origin:* C-0018. *Related:* C-0018, SRC-006. *Status:* open. *Needs:* historical sources.
