# ASN-0006: Conceptual reset

## Rationale (coordinator only)

- The whole v0.1 ledger comes from one investigation in one encoding (DEF-001). This is the program's first deliberate conceptual reset (cycle step G) and addresses DIR-009.
- **Minimal context.** The investigator sees no findings, no preferred representation, and no list of the program's assumptions. A framing-exposed variant (supplying the program's working assumptions as a brief) is a candidate for a later cycle; doing it now would anchor the reset to the current framing.
- **Model:** GPT-6.1 Sol Max. The dominant framing so far came from Claude Opus 5.5, and a reset from a different model family is more likely to escape it.
- *Comparison after registration:* register rediscoveries of ledger results as independent derivations. Findings outside the ledger are blind spots. Disagreement with the program's framing goes into directions or disputes, not dismissed.

## Completion criteria (coordinator only)

- At least one alternative framing developed to a checkable consequence, compared honestly with a baseline.
- On registration: also write a short comparison note in `registration.md` describing how the investigator's default framing relates to DEF-001 and to the ledger.

# Investigation Assignment: Questioning the Framing of the Yijing as a Mathematical Object

You are assigned an independent investigation of how the Yijing should be framed as an object of mathematical study.

Your objective is to identify the framings that investigators would most likely adopt by default and determine which of their assumptions are necessary and which merely customary. Then develop at least one substantially different framing far enough to see whether it reveals structure, constraints, or questions that the default framings obscure.

You have been given only the common research charter, this assignment, a description of the relevant objects or source material, and the research handoff protocol. You have deliberately not been given the findings of other investigators in this program. Work from the supplied material and your general knowledge, and cite any literature you rely on.

You may consider any layer of the material: the figures, their relations, the attested arrangements, the procedures, the texts. You may conclude that the most productive object of study is something other than the set of 64 figures.

**Deliverables.** An explicit statement of the default framing or framings you identify, and their load-bearing assumptions. At least two substantially different alternative framings, each precise enough to yield a checkable consequence. At least one worked result in an alternative framing, with proof, computation, or a clearly marked gap. A comparison of the framings on a consequence where they disagree.

**Useful completion.** At least one alternative framing has been developed to the point of a checkable consequence and honestly compared with a conventional baseline.

At the end, identify which of your claims most warrant independent verification and which questions would be most valuable for another investigator to pursue.

Follow the common research charter and the research handoff protocol.
<!-- END ASSIGNMENT -->
