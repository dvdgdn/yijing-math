# ASN-0007: Source baselines

## Rationale (coordinator only)

- Every source-dependent claim currently inherits an uncited transcription. This is an enabling task, not an independent mathematical verification. It can also unlock neglected sources without choosing their mathematical representation.
- **Intentional overlap:** ASN-0001 and ASN-0002 make independent transcriptions for verification; ASN-0005 also transcribes the received order. This assignment creates a cited comparison baseline. Do not send its tables to the verifiers before their phase 1 is saved. Agreement by copying the baseline is not an independent transcription.
- Covers intake for DIR-005, DIR-006, DIR-007, DIR-008 and DIR-011, not mathematical exploration of those directions. Q-001 and Q-008 receive source leads or documented access limits, not presumed answers.
- Prefer this to another broad exploration of the received order when source access is the limiting resource. Do not let a complete catalogue of traditions become a prerequisite for starting research.

## Completion criteria (coordinator only)

- Cited, explicitly unchecked working transcriptions for the received order and one palace table, or an exact account of inaccessible material and what is needed to obtain it. If access is limited, complete the smallest useful source package first.
- One procedure source for divination if available; a bounded intake list for the textual layer and other arrangements/systems, with access and variant issues.
- Proposed data versions and sidecars follow `data/README.md`. Compare independent tables mechanically after their outputs are saved, resolving differences against the cited source. Do not fabricate checking outcomes or mark model agreement as historical authority.
- For the textual pilot ASN-0009, choose a cited edition or passage and prepare a neutral source excerpt with transcription conventions. Revise its materials before promotion to ready.

# Investigation Assignment: Cited Source Baselines

Establish a small, reusable source basis for mathematical work on the Yijing. Your first deliverables are a full transcription of the received hexagram order and one eight-palace table, each tied to an accessible cited edition or source. Record exact locations, conventions, variants, and how each transcription was checked. Never derive a source table from the mathematical property it will later be used to test.

You have been given neutral object descriptions, the current source registry and data conventions, and methodology. Source descriptions in the registry are placeholders until checked. You have not been given research findings. Do not try to reconstruct them.

Also look for an identifiable source of the palace generation-line mnemonic, recording what can be established about its attestation and whether it is transmitted independently of palace tables. Unknown is acceptable; distinguish a source lead from a historical conclusion.

After the first useful source package, identify one accessible source for a divination procedure and a bounded list of sources for textual analysis, other arrangements, and other correlative systems. For each, state the smallest extract or dataset that could support a checkable investigation, what is accessible, what is uncertain, and which variants matter. Do not attempt an exhaustive bibliography.

**Deliverables.** Transcriptions with source citations and provenance; a source-access and variant log; a short list of next data packages. If a source cannot be accessed, report that limitation and the exact missing input. State whether citations were consulted directly or obtained secondhand.

**Useful completion.** At least one usable cited source package, or a precise access barrier and feasible acquisition path. No mathematical pattern or historical intention needs to be found.

Follow the common research charter and the research handoff protocol.
