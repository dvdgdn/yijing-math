# ASN-0009: Textual pilot

## Rationale (coordinator only)

- DIR-007 is untouched by the registered output and by cycle 1's focused assignments. This opens a distinct material layer rather than merely changing the mathematical vocabulary for the same objects.
- Draft until ASN-0007 supplies a cited source excerpt and conventions. Add only neutral source material to `materials`, check the package again, then seek coordinator approval for ready. An intake result is not exploration coverage.
- ASN-0006 may independently choose text in its unrestricted reset. Compare that report before launching this pilot; narrow the pilot or explicitly justify any replication if needed. Shared direction tags alone do not establish shared work.
- Start with a small passage or single textual layer. A full corpus project would make source and normalization work dominate before the value of the question is known.

## Completion criteria (coordinator only)

- Source excerpt and transcription choices are fixed before launch; no prior patterns or failed hypotheses appear in the package.
- A well-posed mathematical question, explicit annotation/normalization choices, a feasible baseline, and one worked example or a precise reason the data cannot support the question.
- At reconciliation distinguish pilot feasibility, exploratory observations, and future confirmatory work. A pilot need not find a positive pattern to complete usefully.

# Investigation Assignment: Mathematical Questions About a Textual Layer

Use the cited source excerpt supplied with this assignment to investigate what checkable mathematical questions its textual structure can support. Keep the textual layer, variant readings, and any normalization or annotation choices explicit. Work with the source language where possible; state what depends on a translation.

You have deliberately not been given earlier findings of this program. Choose representations because they clarify a question about the supplied text. Consider more than one approach, but develop only the most informative feasible one into a small worked example.

Before interpreting a numerical pattern, state a baseline that preserves the relevant constraints of the material and identify which choices could manufacture the pattern. Label all pilot analyses exploratory. If the excerpt is too small or ambiguous for a proposed test, show why and specify the additional material needed.

**Deliverables.** An exact question; source, normalization and annotation record; a worked mathematical example with evidence or a feasibility limitation; a compact search log; and the cheapest next check with a stopping condition.

**Useful completion.** A successor can tell whether to develop this question, change the source or representation, or stop this formulation. Positive statistical evidence is not required.

Follow the common research charter and the research handoff protocol.
