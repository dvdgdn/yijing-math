# Investigation Assignment: Local Changes and Joint Ordering Structure

Study the supplied coordinate-local permutation model as an explicit mathematical comparison mechanism. Its individual-label marginals become uniform before the whole order becomes uniform. Develop one consequence that detects or constrains the remaining joint structure.

Fix a bounded operation grammar and depth or description budget before searching for a statistic. Validate a tractable small-dimensional case exactly, then state what follows for six-coordinate positions and for a parameterized family. Seek a finite obstruction, a classification at bounded depth, or a joint-information measure whose interpretation differs from one-card marginals.

Read the source's upper-bound quantifiers carefully. An asymptotic result is not a finite numerical mixing certificate. If a constant cannot be extracted, retain that limitation and use a separately justified finite comparison. Do not fit an operation set to a target order and then treat generatability as explanatory evidence.

**Deliverables.** Exact mechanism and budget; complete small reference calculation; a new joint consequence and baseline; finite/asymptotic scope; and the minimum cited arrangement data needed for a later source-dependent test.

**Useful completion.** One checkable joint constraint or a precise explanation of why the selected mechanism cannot discriminate the proposed structures. Merely rederiving the supplied one-sweep support bound is insufficient.
