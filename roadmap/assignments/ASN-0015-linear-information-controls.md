# Coding-theoretic comparison objects

## Rationale (coordinator only)

A full-context review found a balanced parity label retaining less information than the generation label. Establish what this teaches about the distinction between endpoint coefficients and full information, while the isolated verifiers work. No verdict from this investigation is independent verification. Keep its results out of both verifiers' phase-1 workspaces.

## Completion criteria

1. A reproducible counterexample to the broad least-informative reading of the endpoint statement, with an analytic comparison near both endpoints.
2. A proof or exact obstruction for the coordinate benchmark restricted to linear labels; distinguish this restriction from the open all-balanced-label question.
3. Exhaustive classification of rank-three linear labels on six uniform bits at coin noise, using exact arithmetic for any extremal verdict. Broader all-noise optimality stays conjectural unless proved.
4. Bounded prior-art search and explicit novelty limitations.
